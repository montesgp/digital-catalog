# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to a community that is welcoming, inclusive, and free from harassment,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or any
other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different viewpoints.
- Give and receive feedback with empathy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help improve the project.
- Focus discussion on what benefits the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized content or attention, insults, discrimination,
doxxing, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting
are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents this project
publicly. The repository maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions or
communications that do not meet these standards. Reports are handled promptly,
fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

Report an incident privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub rather
than opening a public issue with personal information.

## Enforcement guidelines

Responses may range from a private correction to a warning, temporary ban, or
permanent ban, depending on the impact and pattern of conduct.
